WOF Testing

Your Warrant of Fitness is due — don't stress. Storer Motors is a fully certified WOF inspection station. Our experienced inspectors will have your vehicle checked quickly and thoroughly, so you can get back on the road with confidence.

Our WOF inspection covers:

  • Lights & electrical
  • Brakes
  • Steering & suspension
  • Tyres & wheels
  • Windscreen & visibility
  • Chassis & body
  • Safety equipment
