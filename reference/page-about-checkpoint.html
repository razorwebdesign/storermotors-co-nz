Our Story

Storer Motors has been operating for over 50 years, since opening in 1966 by CEO Reg Storer. What started as a small mechanical garage has grown into one of Christchurch's most respected used vehicle dealerships.

Today, Storer Motors employs more than 10 people, operating both a car sales yard and a full mechanic workshop in New Brighton. Each year, we help customers from all around Christchurch and New Zealand — upgrading, downsizing, or simply keeping their beloved family vehicle in tip-top shape.

Our Values

Customer First

Storer Motors prides itself on exceptional customer service. Every team member treats every customer as if they were family — because that's what we believe in.

Community Roots

We're deeply embedded in the New Brighton community. Most of our customers come back time and again — and we've helped generations of the same families over the years.

Honest & Transparent

No hidden costs, no surprises. We tell you exactly what's needed, what it'll cost, and why — before we do anything.

Quality You Can Trust

Unlike other car yards, Reg personally travels to Japan to purchase each vehicle. He keeps the customer in mind on every purchase, looking for vehicles he can personally endorse.

Once a car arrives at our yard, our workshop gives it a complete service — from adjusting tyre pressures to replacing timing belts — so you can drive away with total confidence.
